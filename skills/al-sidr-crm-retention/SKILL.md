# AL SIDR CRM & Retention

Optimize repeat purchase without inventing customer attributes or bypassing
consent and channel rules.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the data source, consent state, channel eligibility and measurement window.
2. Segment from observed behavior: first order, repeat buyer, category affinity,
   recency/frequency/value, lapsed customer, high-value customer and gift buyer.
3. Prefer deterministic lifecycle triggers before speculative AI scoring.
4. Build flows for welcome, post-purchase education, replenishment, cross-sell,
   winback, VIP/corporate follow-up and review requests where permitted.
5. Keep wellness messaging conservative; never infer health conditions or use
   sensitive personal data for targeting.
6. Define holdouts or baseline metrics where possible: revenue per recipient,
   repeat rate, unsubscribe rate, conversion, AOV and incremental lift.
7. Verify suppression/consent before send or publish actions.

## Output
- Audience definition
- Trigger and eligibility
- EN/AR message strategy
- Offer rule if approved
- Measurement plan
- Consent/claims checks
- Draft / approval boundary / next action
