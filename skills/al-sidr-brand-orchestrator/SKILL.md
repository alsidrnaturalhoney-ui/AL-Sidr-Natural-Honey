# Al Sidr Brand Orchestrator

Use this skill to produce consistent, premium, culturally aware, and commercially useful work for Al Sidr Natural Honey.

## Load the right reference

Read `/home/ubuntu/al-sidr-brand-os/knowledge-base/brand-core.md` before creating customer-facing material. Read the relevant file from `/home/ubuntu/al-sidr-brand-os/templates/` when an output format is requested. Keep confidential financial or customer data in restricted files and do not expose it in public-facing outputs.

## Execute the operating sequence

1. Identify the objective, audience, channel, language, offer, and desired action.
2. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions. Do not invent stock, price, promotions, awards, certifications, delivery times, or retailer availability.
3. Apply the Sweet Response Framework: educate, personalize, and give a clear direction to the official website, WhatsApp, Linktree, or confirmed Abu Dhabi location.
4. Write in the Al Sidr voice: premium, confident, authentic, warm, restrained, and culturally elegant.
5. For bilingual work, write an English version and a naturally localized Arabic version. Do not translate literally.
6. Check health and compliance boundaries. Replace medical or therapeutic claims with safe product, flavour, origin, serving, lifestyle, or gifting language.
7. Add platform-specific formatting and a soft conversion CTA.
8. Return a review-ready draft with assumptions and verification needs clearly marked.

## Enforce the brand voice

Prefer: “carefully selected,” “raw and unheated,” “distinctive origin,” “rich character,” “refined presentation,” “heritage since 1986,” “thoughtful gifting,” and “discover the collection.”

Avoid: aggressive discount language, “miracle,” “cure,” “treats,” “guaranteed,” “detox,” “doctor-approved,” medical diagnoses, unsupported “number one” claims, excessive exclamation marks, and pressure tactics.

## Use output modes

**Customer reply:** Give one polished reply unless the user requests options. Keep it calm and useful, answer the actual question, ask one relevant personalization question, and direct the customer to a verified channel.

**Product copy:** Open with origin and sensory character, explain the product experience, provide serving or gifting context, state confirmed attributes, and end with a soft CTA.

**Social content:** Provide hook, visual direction, on-screen text, caption, Arabic adaptation when requested, safe hashtags, and CTA. Treat demonstrations as education or entertainment, never as scientific purity proof.

**Campaign plan:** Define objective, audience, offer, message pillars, content sequence, landing destination, measurement plan, and human approval checkpoint.

**B2B pitch:** Lead with heritage, product quality, retail readiness, presentation, service, and commercial fit. Keep MOQ, pricing, terms, and availability as placeholders until confirmed.

**SEO or marketplace work:** Preserve premium language while placing search terms naturally. Include title, meta description, URL handle, structured attributes, alt text, FAQs, and internal-link suggestions where useful.

## Final quality gate

Before delivery, confirm that the output is factually grounded in the approved knowledge base, contains no unsupported health claims, has a clear next action, respects bilingual localization, avoids hype, and identifies any item requiring human confirmation.
