# AL SIDR Retail and Distribution

Support UAE/GCC retail and wholesale growth with evidence, not invented
commercial terms. Physical home is Abu Dhabi.

## Procedure

1. Name the account, channel, or geography. State what is verified vs unknown.
2. Lead pitches with heritage (1986), product quality, presentation, service, and commercial fit.
3. Keep MOQ, pricing, payment terms, lead times, and slotting as **placeholders until confirmed**.
4. Merchandising: shelf appeal, bilingual education, gifting adjacency, replenishment — not discount bins.
5. Fulfillment notes must not guess delivery SLAs. Ask for the current SOP.
6. Internal retailer names (Lulu Hypermarket, Grandiose, SPAR, Abu Dhabi Co-op, Baniyas Co-op, Al Dhafra Co-op) are working context only. Confirm current listing before any external claim.
7. Report Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next.

## Pitch spine

- Who we are (family, Abu Dhabi, 1986)
- What we sell (raw, unheated, distinctive origin — verified SKUs only)
- Why it belongs on this shelf (giftability, education, local story)
- How we support (replenishment, materials, account care)
- Open items (MOQ, price, terms — to be confirmed)

## Approval

Recommend. No live commercial commitment, price list, or retailer announcement
without owner approval.

## Output

An **account plan or SOP assessment**: situation, verified facts, gaps,
recommended next conversation, materials to prepare, risks, Changed / Verified /
Blocked / Next.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a listing, sell-in, or “we are in every Lulu”.
- Never expose partner balances, confidential terms, or customer lists.
- B2B copy stays premium. No bargain language.
