# AL SIDR Content Engine

Turn one approved idea into a channel-ready draft package. Canonical content
planning lives in the Al Sidr Content Calendar — do not create a parallel calendar.

Pipeline: **Input → Processing → Output → Optimization → Human Review**.

## Procedure

1. Capture input: objective, audience, product, offer, channel, language, source of truth.
2. Teach before selling. Use the Sweet Response Framework.
3. Write English; localize Arabic separately when requested.
4. Package for the channel (see below). Add a soft CTA to a confirmed destination.
5. Claims and availability check. Mark anything that needs human confirmation.
6. Do not publish. Hand off a review-ready package.

## Channel notes

- **TikTok / Reels:** tactile pours, breakfast pairings, origin, packaging, founder education. Hook in first 2 seconds.
- **Instagram:** heritage, product education, gifting, FAQs, refined lifestyle.
- **YouTube:** longer origin, tasting, founder, retail, behind-the-scenes.
- **Email / CRM:** one idea, one CTA, no invented promotions.
- **Blog / SEO:** pair with the bilingual SEO skill; preserve premium voice.

Each concept includes: objective, audience, hook, visual direction, script or
on-screen text, caption, EN/AR when requested, CTA, destination, safe hashtags,
production notes, KPI.

## Recurring series (propose, do not invent history)

Origin stories · tasting notes · gifting occasions · texture/crystallisation
education · Abu Dhabi heritage · pairing ideas. Build series, not one-offs.

## Approval

Draft / publish with approval. Escalate any live posting, boosting, or creator partnership.

## Guardrails

- No medical claims. No fake purity tests.
- No invented prices, stock, discounts, or retailer lists.
- Confirm awards, tags, and partnerships before they appear on-screen.
- Direct to https://alsidrnaturalhoney.com or WhatsApp 0529830095, not generic “link in bio” without the real destination.

## Output

A **channel-ready draft package** plus Review Notes and Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next.
