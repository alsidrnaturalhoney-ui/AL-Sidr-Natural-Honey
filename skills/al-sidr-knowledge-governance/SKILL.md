# AL SIDR Knowledge Governance

Convert scattered AL SIDR material into a governed, searchable knowledge record
without creating a parallel knowledge system.

## Knowledge domains

Business and heritage · Products and commerce · SEO and growth · Brand and
creative · Operations and retail · Technology and security · AI and automation.

Canonical evidence lives in approved brand narrative, Shopify live catalog,
verified analytics, approved visual system, approved SOPs, GitHub, and the
Notion Canonical Registry.

## Procedure

1. Discover the material. Capture title, location, owner, date, and domain.
2. Classify into one domain. If it spans domains, pick a primary and link the rest.
3. Deduplicate against the canonical registry. Prefer the newest complete operational asset.
4. Canonicalize: one record, one owner, one review cadence, one archive path.
5. Mark freshness: current / aging / stale / unverified. Do not treat stale pages as facts.
6. Retrieval note: how an agent should find this later (canonical URL, not a copy).
7. Report Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next.

## Approval

Recommend / draft. Inventory and classification are allowed.
Escalate: move, archive, or migrate records.

## Output

A **governed knowledge record**:

- Title and domain
- Canonical location (link, do not duplicate the body elsewhere)
- Owner and review cadence
- Freshness
- Related duplicates and what to archive after dependency check
- Facts vs assumptions vs gaps
- Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next

## Guardrails

- Never invent business facts, prices, certifications, lab results, or awards.
- Keep confidential financial, customer, and partner data out of public pages.
- Link to the system of record; do not paste live catalog or customer data into Notion as a second copy.
- Decision hierarchy: verified company data > catalog data > official certificates > live system data > approved brand guidelines > approved marketing > trusted external sources > model knowledge.
