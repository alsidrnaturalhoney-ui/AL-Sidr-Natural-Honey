# Al Sidr Bilingual Copy

Read `/home/ubuntu/al-sidr-brand-os/knowledge-base/brand-core.md` first. Treat English and Arabic as parallel brand expressions, not literal translations.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, channel, product, objective, offer, and CTA.
2. Verify product attributes and mark unknowns instead of inventing them.
3. Write the English version in a premium, clear, warm, and restrained voice.
4. Localize the Arabic version for UAE/GCC comprehension and cultural fluency.
5. Check that both versions preserve the same meaning, factual boundaries, and desired action.
6. Add a concise reviewer note for claims, dates, prices, links, or availability requiring confirmation.

## Preferred structure

Use explicit headers: `English Version`, `Arabic Version`, and `Review Notes`. For product copy, lead with origin and sensory character, explain the experience, add serving or gifting context, and close with a soft CTA. For ads, produce a hook, body, CTA, and platform-safe text length.

## Guardrails

Do not use medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, cure, detox, guaranteed, or unsupported certification claims. Avoid cheap discount language and excessive punctuation. Use approved destinations from the brand knowledge base, especially the official website and WhatsApp channel.
