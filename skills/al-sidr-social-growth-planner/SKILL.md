# Al Sidr Social Growth Planner

Read `/home/ubuntu/al-sidr-brand-os/knowledge-base/brand-core.md` before planning. Use the pipeline **Input → Processing → Output → Optimization → Human Review**.

## Platform directions

For TikTok and Reels, prioritize tactile macro shots, honey pours, breakfast pairings, origin storytelling, packaging details, and founder-led education. For Instagram, combine heritage, product education, gifting, customer questions, and refined lifestyle imagery. For YouTube, develop deeper origin, tasting, founder, retail, and behind-the-scenes narratives.

## Required content brief

For each concept provide: objective, audience, hook, visual direction, spoken or on-screen script, caption, English and Arabic copy when requested, CTA, destination, safe hashtags, production notes, and KPI.

## Growth principles

Teach before asking for a sale. Use comments and DMs to personalize recommendations. Direct qualified interest to the official website, WhatsApp, Linktree, or confirmed Abu Dhabi location. Build recurring series rather than isolated posts. Test hooks, formats, thumbnails, posting windows, and CTAs one variable at a time.

## Guardrails

Do not present informal water, matchstick, or flow demonstrations as scientific purity tests. Do not make medical or therapeutic promises. Confirm current awards, tags, retailer availability, prices, promotions, and creator partnerships before publishing. Require human review before posting.
