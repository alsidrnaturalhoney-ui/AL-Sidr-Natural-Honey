# AL SIDR Shopify Growth

Shopify is the live commerce and catalog source of truth. Audit and propose.
Do not mutate price, stock, publish state, or archives without approval.

## Procedure

1. Read the live catalog before recommending. If Shopify is not readable, stop and report Blocked.
2. Identify the product, collection, or funnel step. Prefer the newest complete listing as canonical.
3. Separate verified catalog facts from assumptions.
4. For duplicates: keep the canonical listing, list URL/collection/campaign dependencies, recommend archive — do not archive.
5. Draft a change plan: what, why, evidence, risk, rollback, approval needed.
6. CRO notes must be specific (title, media, variant clarity, trust, delivery, gifting, bilingual). No generic “add urgency”.
7. Draft-only products tagged bundle-only / negative-margin-standalone stay drafts.
8. Report Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next.

## Known duplicate pattern

Kashmiri Sidr: prefer the newer multi-variant listing (250g/500g/1KG) as likely
canonical; the older single-variant 500g listing is an archive candidate after
URL and collection checks. Re-verify live before acting.

## Output

A **verified commerce change plan**:

- Scope (product / collection / theme / checkout)
- Current verified state
- Proposed change
- Copy drafts (EN / AR if needed)
- CRO hypothesis and how to measure
- Risk and rollback
- Approval required
- Changed / Verified / Blocked / Next

## Approval

Draft / execute with approval. Audit and proposals are allowed.
Escalate: price, stock, publish, unpublish, archive, redirects, theme deploy.

## Guardrails

- Never invent SKUs, prices, promotions, inventory, delivery times, or reviews.
- Never fabricate medical or purity-test claims on product pages.
- Bundle-only drafts must not be published as standalone products.
- Preserve SEO URLs. Propose redirects rather than silent deletes.
