# AL SIDR Creative Production System

## Objective
One unified production workflow for premium AL SIDR Natural Honey e-commerce and social media creative.

## Brand System
- AL SIDR Natural Honey Est. 1986
- Luxury heritage, trustworthy, professional
- Gold, cream, charcoal black
- Subtle honeycomb texture
- Premium glass jar presentation
- UAE heritage cues
- English/Arabic readiness
- Clean, cinematic, premium FMCG aesthetic

## Production Pipeline
Brief → Creative Strategy → Script/Storyboard → Asset Selection → Video Production → Design Adaptations → English/Arabic Localization → QA → Approval → Publishing → Performance Tracking → Reuse

## Tool Roles
- Lovable: unified creative production control app and workflow orchestration
- InVideo: scalable video production and editing pipeline
- HeyGen: avatar narration, lip-sync, localization and presenter workflows
- Canva: reusable social design system and campaign variants
- Adobe: high-end creative editing, asset refinement and finishing
- Adobe Express: rapid campaign and social variations
- Adobe Acrobat: PDFs, documents, approvals and production packs
- Gamma: strategy decks, briefs, presentations and creative planning
- Notion: knowledge base, SOPs, campaign calendar and production tracking
- GitHub: source control, specifications, version history and engineering documentation

## Governance
- Never invent product claims, certifications, awards, ingredients, nutrition facts or performance data.
- Preserve brand consistency across every output.
- Prefer reusable templates and modular assets.
- Design mobile-first for social channels.
- Create English and Arabic variants when appropriate.
- Track source assets, versions, approvals and final exports.

## Master Assets
- Canva master social hero
- Canva vertical Story master
- Canva YouTube thumbnail master
- Canva Creative Production System specification
- InVideo master creative project

## Future Integration Contract
The Lovable AL SIDR AI Hub should expose a Creative Studio module that routes briefs to the appropriate production tool, stores campaign metadata, links final assets, records approvals, and reports performance back to the executive dashboard.
